# Final Cut Pro: 30-Day Plan (Learn FCP and Post to YouTube Daily)

**Goal:** By day 30 you can edit a long video and a Short quickly in Final Cut Pro (FCP), and you upload to YouTube every day.

**Daily time:** about 1.5 to 2 hours.
- 30 to 45 min: learn (a video lesson plus the matching manual chapter)
- 45 to 60 min: make one Short or video
- 15 min: export, upload, write notes

**Rule:** learn a skill, then use it in that day's upload. Publishing every day is the practice.

---

## Video or presentation/manual: which is better?

Use **video lessons to learn** and the **manual to look things up**.

- FCP is a hands-on tool. It's easier to learn the magnetic timeline, dragging, trimming and keyboard shortcuts by watching someone do it.
- The **FCP User Guide (PDF)** is the most accurate and complete source. Don't read it cover to cover. After each video, read the matching chapter (listed below) to fill gaps and check details.
- Slides or presentations are the weakest choice for editing software. Only use them for a quick summary of shortcuts.

**Method:** watch a 10 to 20 min lesson, then copy it right away in FCP with your own footage, then check the manual chapter, then publish.

## Setup (Day 0, before you start)

- Create a YouTube channel. Verify it at youtube.com/verify so you can upload videos over 15 minutes and use custom thumbnails.
- Pick a **niche** and **one repeatable format** (for example "1 tip in 45 seconds", a daily vlog or a tutorial). Daily uploads are only realistic with a format you can repeat.
- Make folders for Footage, Music (from the YouTube Audio Library, which is copyright-safe), Graphics and Exports.
- Shoot on your phone in 4K or 1080p at 30fps. Keep a stock of 5 to 10 clips so you never start from zero.

## Week 1: Core editing (Days 1–7)
*Upload: one simple Short every day (vertical, 15 to 60 sec).*

| Day | Learn | Manual chapter (User Guide) | Publish |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Interface tour: libraries, events, projects, browser, viewer, timeline | "Intro to Final Cut Pro" / "Interface" | Short: talking-head intro to your channel |
| 2 | Import media, organise with keywords and favourites, create a **vertical project (1080×1920)** | "Import media", "Organize media" | Short |
| 3 | Magnetic timeline: append (E), insert (W), connect (Q), overwrite (D), blade (B / Cmd+B) | "Add and edit clips" | Short |
| 4 | Trimming: ripple, roll, slip and slide, the precision editor, J‑K‑L playback | "Trim clips" | Short with tight jump cuts |
| 5 | Audio basics: levels, fades, Voice Isolation, the Enhance options | "Edit audio" | Short with music under the voice |
| 6 | Titles and **automatic captions** (Transcribe to Captions in FCP 11+) | "Add titles", "Captions" | Short with burned-in captions |
| 7 | **Export:** Share > Apple Devices / YouTube & Facebook, H.264, 1080p. Upload on YouTube Studio | "Share projects" | Short, plus a review of your week |

**End of Week 1 check:** you can make a 30-second captioned Short in under 45 minutes.

## Daily "real-time" workflow (once you're fluent)

1. **Shoot (10 min):** phone, vertical, good light, clip-on mic.
2. **Import (2 min):** AirDrop to Mac, then FCP, then your keyword collection.
3. **Edit (15 min):** duplicate your template project, rough cut with blade and delete, Transcribe to Captions, add music at -20 to -25 dB under voice.
4. **Polish (5 min):** Enhance Light and Color, Voice Isolation, a hook title in the first 2 seconds.
5. **Export (3 min):** Share > YouTube (or Master File H.264), vertical 1080×1920.
6. **Upload (5 min):** title with keywords, add #Shorts, schedule a consistent time.

> **Tip:** Apple's **Final Cut Camera** (free iPhone app) and **Final Cut Pro for iPad** can speed up shooting and on-the-go edits, if you have the devices.

## Must-know shortcuts

| Key | Action | Key | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| E | Append to timeline | B | Blade tool |
| Q | Connect clip | Cmd+B | Blade at playhead |
| W | Insert | A | Select tool |
| D | Overwrite | T | Trim tool |
| J / K / L | Reverse / Stop / Play | I / O | Set in / out |
| Cmd+T | Default transition | Ctrl+T | Default title (lower third) |
| Opt+[ / Opt+] | Trim start / end to playhead | Shift+Z | Fit timeline |
| Cmd+R | Retime menu | Cmd+E | Export (default share) |
| N | Snapping on/off | V | Enable/disable clip |
