# Final Cut Pro: 30-Day Plan (Learn FCP and Post to YouTube Daily)

**Goal:** By day 30 you can edit a long video and a Short quickly in Final Cut Pro (FCP), and you upload to YouTube every day.

**Daily time:** about 1.5 to 2 hours.
- 30 to 45 min: learn (a video lesson plus the matching manual pages)
- 45 to 60 min: make one Short or video
- 15 min: export, upload, write notes

**Rule:** learn a skill, then use it in that day's upload. Publishing every day is the practice.

> **Page numbers** come from your *Final Cut Pro User Guide for Mac* (FCP 12.4, 977 pages). They are the guide's own printed page numbers ("p." means page).

---

## Video or presentation/manual: which is better?

Use **video lessons to learn** and the **manual to look things up**.

- FCP is a hands-on tool. It's easier to learn the magnetic timeline, dragging, trimming and keyboard shortcuts by watching someone do it.
- The **User Guide** is the most accurate and up-to-date source. Don't read all 977 pages. After each video, read only the pages listed for that day.
- Slides or presentations are the weakest choice for editing software. Only use them for a quick summary of shortcuts.

**Method:** watch a 10 to 20 min lesson, then copy it right away in FCP with your own footage, then read the listed pages, then publish.

## Your 30-day free plan: Apple Creator Studio

Your manual says FCP is now part of **Apple Creator Studio** (p. 16–18). It's a subscription that includes Final Cut Pro, **Motion, Compressor, Pixelmator Pro** (useful for thumbnails) and Logic Pro, plus extra downloadable titles, transitions and generators (p. 18).

- Before day 30, decide whether to continue. If you cancel, you keep access until the end of the billing period (p. 18). Manage it in **Final Cut Pro > Manage Subscriptions**.
- Requirements: macOS 26.6 or later (p. 6). **Generate Captions** (automatic subtitles) needs an **Apple silicon Mac** and works in **U.S. English only** (p. 385).
- Use the extra apps during the trial: make thumbnails in Pixelmator Pro (p. 828) and use the extra title templates (p. 18).

## Setup (Day 0, before you start)

- Create a YouTube channel. Verify it at youtube.com/verify so you can upload videos over 15 minutes and use custom thumbnails.
- Pick a **niche** and **one repeatable format** (for example "1 tip in 45 seconds", a daily vlog or a tutorial). Daily uploads are only realistic with a format you can repeat.
- Make folders for Footage, Music (from the YouTube Audio Library, which is copyright-safe), Graphics and Exports.
- Shoot on your phone in 4K or 1080p at 30fps. Keep a stock of 5 to 10 clips so you never start from zero. Apple's free **Final Cut Camera** iPhone app works with FCP (p. 42–43).

## Daily "real-time" workflow (once you're fluent)

1. **Shoot (10 min):** phone or Final Cut Camera, vertical, good light, clip-on mic.
2. **Import (2 min):** AirDrop to Mac, then FCP, then your keyword collection.
3. **Edit (15 min):** duplicate your template project, rough cut with blade and delete, Generate Captions, add music at -20 to -25 dB under voice.
4. **Polish (5 min):** Enhance Light and Color, Enhance Audio / Voice Isolation, a hook title in the first 2 seconds.
5. **Export (3 min):** Share > **Social Platforms**, with captions burned in for Shorts.
6. **Upload (5 min):** in YouTube Studio, title with keywords, add #Shorts, schedule a consistent time.

## Must-know shortcuts (full list: p. 897–923)

| Key | Action | Key | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| E | Append to timeline | B | Blade tool |
| Q | Connect clip | Cmd+B | Blade at playhead |
| W | Insert | A | Select tool |
| D | Overwrite | T | Trim tool |
| J / K / L | Reverse / Stop / Play | I / O | Set in / out |
| Cmd+T | Default transition | Ctrl+T | Default title (lower third) |
| Opt+[ / Opt+] | Trim start / end to playhead | Shift+Z | Fit timeline |
| Cmd+R | Retime menu | Cmd+E | Export (default share) |
| N | Snapping on/off | V | Enable/disable clip |
| Opt+Cmd+A | Enhance Audio | Cmd+Opt+K | Command Editor |
